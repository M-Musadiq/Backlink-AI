Session Configuration Instructions

Option 1: Auto Login capturing

Uses automated browser capture. Perfect for quick setups.

  1. Click Auto Login on a platform card.
  2. An automated browser window will launch on your system.
  3. Manually complete the login steps on the platform.
  4. Close the window or wait — cookies are automatically captured.
Option 2: Import Cookie JSON

Captures cookies directly from your active browser session.

  1. Install the Cookie Editor extension in Chrome.
  2. Log in to the destination website in Chrome manually.
  3. Open Cookie Editor → click Export as JSON.
  4. Click Import here, paste the JSON, and confirm.
